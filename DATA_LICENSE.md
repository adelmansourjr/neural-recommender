# Data attribution and use

This repository's MIT license applies to its original software. It does not
relicense MovieLens data or grant commercial rights to that data or trained
artifacts.

Source: [MovieLens 1M, GroupLens Research](https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/1m/).
The [upstream README](https://files.grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/ml-1m-README.txt)
permits research use with acknowledgment, prohibits endorsement claims, and
requires separate permission for redistribution and commercial/revenue-bearing
uses. Raw data is excluded from Git. Download it from GroupLens with the supplied
command. Review the upstream terms before publishing a commercial demonstration
or redistributing artifacts derived from it.

Citation: F. Maxwell Harper and Joseph A. Konstan. 2015. The MovieLens Datasets:
History and Context. ACM Transactions on Interactive Intelligent Systems 5(4),
Article 19. https://doi.org/10.1145/2827872

Downloads retain a SHA-256 receipt locally. The report records that data digest,
and serving verifies it against the model's catalog metadata. MovieLens 1M has
1,000,209 ratings, 6,040 users, and 3,883 movie records; movie titles and genres
are available, not full plot summaries. No endorsement by GroupLens or the
University of Minnesota is implied.
